
## Business Questions

This analysis aims to answer the following business questions:

1. How are sales and profit changing over time?
2. Which regions contribute the most to overall profit?
3. Who are the top-performing customers?
4. Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
5. How are sales and profit distributed across product sub-categories?
6. Which cities and states contribute most to sales performance?

## Dashboard Features

- Overall Sales, Profit, COGS and Orders KPIs
- Year-wise Sales and Profit Trends
- Region-wise Profit Analysis
- Top 10 Customer Analysis
- Top Product Performance
- Sub-category Distribution
- Top City and State Analysis
- Interactive Region and Segment Filters
